Privacy Policy

Effective: October 17, 2026 Last updated: October 17, 2026

Sojito is designed to keep your planning data private. We do not sell personal information, serve advertising, track you across apps or websites, or use third-party analytics SDKs.

This Privacy Policy explains how AdoboIO ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Sojito application, including its iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, widget, and complication experiences (collectively, the "App").

1. Information handled by the App

Goals and preferences. The App processes information you choose to enter, such as goal titles, notes, target dates, dashboard visibility settings, and countdown preferences. This information is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is available, goal information is also synchronized through your private iCloud database so it can be available to your devices.

Location for weather. If you grant location permission, the App requests your location to display current weather for your area. The App uses foreground, when-in-use location access and does not use location for advertising or cross-app tracking. Location information is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to fulfill your weather request. We do not maintain our own location history or database.

Subscription information. If you view, purchase, or restore Sojito Plus, the transaction is processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. The App receives product and entitlement information needed to provide subscription features, such as the product identifier and whether access is active. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Device storage and sharing between Sojito components. The App uses Apple-provided on-device storage, shared app-group storage, and Watch Connectivity to share goals and preferences among the Sojito app, widgets, and Apple Watch experiences on your devices.

2. How information is used

Information handled by the App is used only to:

  • display and manage your goals, countdowns, widgets, and watch complications;
  • synchronize your goals across your Apple devices through iCloud;
  • provide weather information when you request or enable it;
  • process purchases, restore purchases, and determine subscription access;
  • maintain the security, reliability, and core operation of the App; and
  • respond to privacy or support inquiries that you send to us.

3. Tracking and advertising

Sojito does not contain advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Service providers and disclosures

The App relies on Apple platform services to provide its functionality:

  • Apple iCloud and CloudKit store and synchronize goal information in your private iCloud database.
  • Apple WeatherKit and Core Location provide location-based weather information. Apple states that WeatherKit location information is used only to provide forecasts, is not associated with personally identifiable information, and is not tracked between requests.
  • Apple StoreKit and the App Store process purchases, subscriptions, billing, and restorations.
  • Apple Watch Connectivity and app groups transfer or share App information among Sojito components on your Apple devices.

Apple handles information under its own terms and privacy practices. You can review the Apple Privacy Policy and App Store & Privacy.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the App, or others. Because goal records are stored in each user's private iCloud database, we do not have ordinary direct access to those records through the App.

5. Data retention and deletion

Goal information remains on your device and, when enabled, in your private iCloud database until you delete it, remove the App and its local data, disable or delete the relevant iCloud data, or otherwise manage it through Apple services. Deleting a goal in Sojito removes it from the App's local store and the App is designed to synchronize that deletion with its CloudKit records.

App preferences remain on your device or in shared app-group storage until they are changed, the App's data is removed, or the relevant apps are uninstalled. Subscription transaction records are retained by Apple according to Apple's policies and legal obligations.

6. Your choices and controls

  • You may deny or revoke location access at any time in your device's Settings. Weather features may then be unavailable, but goal and countdown features will continue to work.
  • You may create, edit, and delete goals within the App.
  • You may manage iCloud access in your Apple device settings.
  • You may manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings.
  • You may contact us about a privacy request using the contact information below.

7. Security

We use the safeguards provided by Apple's application sandbox, data-protection technologies, iCloud, CloudKit, StoreKit, and related platform security features. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International processing

Apple services may process information in countries or regions other than your own, as described in Apple's privacy documentation. Where applicable, information is handled subject to the safeguards provided by Apple and applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

Sojito is not directed to children under 13, or under the equivalent minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can evaluate the request.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information, such as rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. Much of the information used by Sojito is controlled directly through the App, your device, and your iCloud account. For requests concerning information handled directly by Apple, contact Apple through its privacy channels. For requests concerning us, use the contact information below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, legal requirements, or our privacy practices. We will revise the "Last updated" date when changes are made. If a change materially affects how the App handles information, we will provide additional notice when required.
